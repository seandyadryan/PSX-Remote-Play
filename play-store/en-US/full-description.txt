Your console. Your local connection.

PSX Remote Play is an independent Android client designed for local-network Remote Play with your own compatible PS4 or PS5. Find your console, save its address, register using its link-device PIN, and manage your connection from one place.

THIS IS AN EARLY, EXPERIMENTAL LAN VERSION
The current build has passed automated and Android emulator tests. Compatibility with physical PS4/PS5 consoles has not yet been verified. Successful registration, wake-up and streaming on your console are not guaranteed. Please consider this limitation before purchasing. Internet connections and automatic PSN sign-in are not available.

CONNECT ON YOUR LOCAL NETWORK
• Search for PS4/PS5 consoles on the same network, or enter an IP address manually.
• Register with your PSN account ID and the console's temporary 8-digit link-device PIN.
• Save pairing credentials encrypted on your Android device.
• Send a wake-up request to a registered console configured for network wake-up in Rest Mode.
• Start a LAN session using the integrated H.264 video and Opus audio connection engine.

MAKE THE SESSION YOURS
• Create profiles for 720p or 1080p, 30 or 60 fps, and 2–50 Mbps bitrate.
• Enable or disable console audio and adjust controller dead zone.
• Use an Android-compatible gamepad, with on-screen PS, Options and touchpad-click controls.
• Explore a clearly labeled local demo to test controller input without a console. The demo does not stream games or play console audio.

WHAT YOU NEED
• Your own compatible PS4 or PS5; PS4 registration targets firmware 8 or later.
• Remote Play enabled in your console settings.
• An Android 8.0+ device with an arm64-v8a or x86_64 processor.
• Your Android device and console on the same local network.
• Your console account's PSN account ID, entered as a decimal number or Base64 value. Your PSN username or email cannot replace this ID.
• A compatible Android gamepad for gameplay; a complete on-screen gamepad is not included.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
Your PSN password is never requested. Account ID retrieval and PSN login are not provided. Wake-up requires the relevant Rest Mode network settings; a sent packet does not confirm that the console has started. Performance and compatibility depend on your console, firmware, Android device and network. Internet/NAT traversal, HDR, HEVC, picture-in-picture, touchpad gestures, microphone chat and haptics are not included. Games and consoles are not included.

PRIVACY AND TRANSPARENCY
No ads or analytics SDKs. No developer-operated streaming relay. Pairing data is stored locally with Android Keystore-backed encryption. You can delete local pairing from the app. Open-source connection component licenses and source/build information are available inside the app.
